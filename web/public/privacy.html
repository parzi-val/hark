Hark · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

The short version: Hark has no servers of its own. Your notes live on your device and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We never receive, store, or read them.

Hark is a voice-first notes app that shapes spoken thoughts into clean notes and checklists. This policy explains, in plain terms, what data Hark handles and how.

What Hark stores

  • Your notes, tasks, and their contents — created by you, kept locally on your device (in the browser or the app).
  • Your settings — theme, your name, and, if you add one, your AI API key.
  • Voice recordings — captured only while you record, sent for transcription, then discarded. Audio is not retained.

Where your data lives — and who can see it

By default, everything stays on your device. If you turn on Google Drive sync, your notes are stored in your Drive's private application data folder — a hidden area only Hark can reach, inside your own Google account. Sync happens directly between your device and your Drive. Hark runs no backend server and never receives a copy of your notes; the developer cannot read your data.

Google account data

When you connect Google Drive, Hark uses Google Sign-In and requests:

  • drive.appdata — to read and write Hark's own private app-data folder in your Drive. Hark cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • Your email and basic profile — used only to identify your account within the app.

Hark's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This access is used solely to provide Hark's sync feature. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or transfer it to anyone.

AI processing (optional, your key)

If you provide a Groq or OpenAI API key, Hark uses it to transcribe your voice recordings and tidy raw text into clean notes and tasks. To do this, the relevant audio or text is sent to the provider you configured, using your key, and is handled under their privacy policy. Hark does not store or route this data anywhere else. If you don't add a key, nothing is sent to any AI provider.

What Hark does not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No backend that could read, retain, or expose your notes.

Your controls

  • Delete data: deleting a note, or clearing the app's storage / uninstalling, removes it locally. To remove synced data, delete the notes while signed in (the change syncs), or delete Hark's app-data folder from your Google Drive.
  • Disconnect Google: sign out in Settings, and/or revoke Hark's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, your data may be handled by:

  • Google (Drive sync and sign-in) — Google Privacy Policy.
  • Your chosen AI provider (Groq or OpenAI) — governed by their respective policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
